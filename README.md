# Budget vs Actual Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes budgeted and actual expenses over 12 months to evaluate financial performance, identify budget variances, and support business decision-making.

## Objectives

- Compare budgeted and actual expenses.
- Calculate monthly and category-level budget variances.
- Measure variance percentages and budget execution.
- Identify overspending and underspending.
- Provide recommendations for better budget management.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab
- Data Analysis
- Business Analytics

## Key Results

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Budget | 1,762,200 |
| Actual Expenses | 1,718,180 |
| Budget Execution Rate | 97.50% |
| Unspent Budget | 44,020 |
| Highest Overspending Category | Operational Costs |
| Operational Cost Variance | +9,082 (+5.50%) |
| Maintenance Cost Variance | +2,487 (+5.18%) |

## Key Insights

- Overall spending was below the planned budget.
- Operational and maintenance costs exceeded their respective budgets.
- Development costs had the largest underspending amount.
- Five months exceeded their budgets, while seven months remained below budget.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate operational cost overruns.
2. Review maintenance expenses and budget assumptions.
3. Investigate whether underspending reflects genuine savings or delayed activities.
4. Monitor monthly variances to improve future financial planning.

## Visualizations

The notebook includes charts comparing budgeted and actual expenses, monthly spending trends, and budget variances by month and cost category.
